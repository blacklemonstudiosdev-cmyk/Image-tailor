AI create & edit
Generate and edit locally with WebGPU. Strict safety checks run before and after processing; first use downloads the model.
Generation and editing run locally in your browser with WebGPU. For editing only, the source image is temporarily staged in Vercel Blob, fetched back into your browser, and deleted after processing. No AI provider receives your prompt or image. Prompts are screened and every result is checked by an image-safety classifier before it can enter the editor. If safety checks cannot load, processing is blocked. No image is uploaded to a generation server. The safety gate is conservative and can still make mistakes; it is not a guarantee.
